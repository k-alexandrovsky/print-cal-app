Print Calibration

Color-correct photos so prints match your screen

Demo calibration. This correction comes from a simulated printer, not yours. Don't print with it: run analyze.py --profile on your chart photos and publish the profile.

Print previews are simulated from your printer's measurements.

    How to print
    1. Choose photos, then tap Save / Share and save them to Photos (or pick your printing app in the share sheet).
    2. Print the saved copies (named after the printer, e.g. _selphy-cp1500) exactly like the calibration chart: same app, same paper, auto-correction and filters off.
    3. Pick the printer at the top: each one has its own calibration and remembers its own correction strength.
    4. New paper type or changed print settings? Re-calibrate that printer.
